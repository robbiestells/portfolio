Galactic Gridlock

Inferno Figure-8. Take the inside line, climb the overpass, jump the lava chasm, and watch the lava spread. Three laps, four cars, the race runs until you cross the line.

  1. Everyone picks one card from their hand each turn.
  2. All cards flip at once.
  3. Every car moves together. Swerves, weapons and traps happen halfway through each move.
  4. Wreck and you lose a turn while your crew rebuilds the car.
  5. Drive into a car ahead and you stop; it takes the damage.
  6. Fall far behind the leader and you get a Draft bonus to your move, so you can always catch up.
  7. The race ends when you cross the line. Prize money: 1st $500, 2nd $300, 3rd $150, 4th nothing.
  8. Hover or tap any tile, hazard or car to see what it does.
